## Cascaded Tanks with Overflow

Repository paths:

- `Cascaded Tanks with Overflow Datasets/dataBenchmark.csv`
- `Cascaded Tanks with Overflow Datasets/dataBenchmark.mat`
- `Cascaded Tanks with Overflow Datasets/TanksBenchmark.pdf`

Dataset source:

- M. Schoukens, P. Mattsson, T. Wigren, and J.-P. Noël, *Cascaded Tanks Benchmark Combining Soft and Hard Nonlinearities*.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.4121/12960104
- Benchmark page: https://www.nonlinearbenchmark.org/benchmarks/cascaded-tanks

The 4TU dataset record identifies the dataset licence. Preserve its attribution and ShareAlike requirements where applicable. The accompanying publication/PDF may have terms separate from the dataset and should be handled according to its publication source.
